Support

We read every message and usually reply within two working days.

Write to contact@kilny.app. If something went wrong, tell us what you were doing, your phone model, and the app version shown in Settings.

Where are my pieces stored?

On your phone. Kilny does not upload your pieces or photos, so uninstalling the app erases them.

Do I need an account?

No. You need one only to subscribe or to connect an Etsy shop.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your pieces stay. You can still view, search, archive and delete them. Creating and changing pieces needs a subscription.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In your Apple App Store or Google Play account, under Subscriptions. You keep access until the paid period ends.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Account screen in the app and choose Delete account, or write to us and we will do it. See the Privacy Policy for what is removed.
