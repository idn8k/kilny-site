Keep track of every piece you make.

Kilny is a studio app for potters. Follow each piece from idea to finished, know which shelf it's on, and list it on Etsy when it's done.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

Six stages, from idea to finished

Every piece has a stage. Move it forward when you trim, fire or glaze, and move it back when plans change. Kilny keeps the dates, so each piece carries its own history.

Made for the way a studio runs

Know where it is

Give each piece a location, such as the damp room or the bisque shelf. Add your own locations to match your studio.

Reminders you set

When a piece changes stage, ask Kilny to remind you to check on it in a few days. Nothing starts a timer unless you do.

List on Etsy

Connect your Etsy shop and list a finished piece from its own page. When it sells, Kilny marks it sold and moves it to your archive.

Your pieces stay on your phone

Photos, notes and firing details are stored on your device, and you can start without an account. How Kilny handles your data.

Something to read while the kiln cools

Kilny comes with articles about the craft, and many of them can be listened to.

Free for your first 30 days

Every feature is open during the trial. After it, a subscription keeps adding and editing switched on. Without one, your pieces stay with you to view and search.
