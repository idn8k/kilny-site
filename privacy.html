Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Kilny is a pottery studio app. This policy explains what the app and this website collect, where it is kept, and what you can do about it.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Kilny is stored on your phone and is not uploaded to us. That covers your pieces, their photos, notes, firing details, stage history, locations, reminders and favorites. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone.

Because this content lives only on your device, we cannot recover it for you. Uninstalling the app erases it.

What we store when you create an account

You can use Kilny without an account. An account is needed to subscribe and to connect Etsy. If you create one, we store:

  • Your email address, and an identifier from Apple or Google if you sign in with them.
  • A small number of settings, so they follow your account.
  • Your subscription status. Apple and Google handle payment, and we never see your card details.

If you connect Etsy

  • We store which Etsy shop belongs to your Kilny account, and your device's push token, so we can tell you when a piece sells.
  • Your Etsy access tokens stay on your device.
  • Listing details and photos pass through our server on their way from your phone to Etsy. We do not keep them.
  • We count requests per account to stay within Etsy's limits.

Etsy handles the information you send it under its own privacy policy.

When you contact us

A message sent from the app or by email reaches us with your email address, the message, and your app version and operating system version, so we can help.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends us a technical report: what failed, the app version and the device model and operating system. Reports do not include your pieces or photos.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase, for accounts, sign-in and the Etsy connection.
  • Resend, to send sign-in codes and deliver contact messages.
  • Sentry, for crash reports.
  • Apple and Google, for sign-in, subscriptions and push notifications.
  • Etsy, when you connect a shop.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or share it with advertisers.

This website

kilny.app sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The company that hosts it keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses.

Deleting your data

Delete your account from the Account screen in the app. That removes your account, your settings and your Etsy connection from our servers. The app then asks separately whether to erase the pieces on your device. You can also ask us to delete your account by writing to contact@kilny.app.

Children

Kilny is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@kilny.app.
